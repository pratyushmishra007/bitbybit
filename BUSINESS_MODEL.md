# BitByBit - Business Model & Monetization Strategy

> **Comprehensive Revenue Strategy for Educational Coding Platform**

---

## Executive Summary

BitByBit is a multi-tenant SaaS platform designed for educational institutions to teach programming. This document outlines the complete business model, pricing strategies, and profit maximization approaches.

## 9. Monetization Tactics

### 9.1 Maximize Free-to-Paid Conversion

**Triggers:**
- 80% of student limit reached → upgrade prompt
- 90% of executions used → suggest upgrade
- Feature attempt blocked → show tier comparison
- Trial expiration → offer discount

**Conversion Tactics:**
- 14-day free trial of Professional
- First month 50% off
- Annual commitment discounts (20%)
- Referral credits

### 9.2 Expand Existing Customers

**Upsell Triggers:**
- New semester (more students)
- New department interested
- Feature request for higher tier
- Usage growth patterns

**Cross-sell Opportunities:**
- Add-on features
- Training services
- Implementation support
- Content creation

### 9.3 Reduce Churn

**Warning Signs:**
- Decreased login frequency
- Support tickets increasing
- Negative NPS score
- Contract renewal approaching

**Retention Actions:**
- Proactive support outreach
- Feature highlight emails
- Success manager intervention
- Discount offers for at-risk
